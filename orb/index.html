02 · EVALUATE

Does the AI really do its job?

AI agents don't just chat. They take actions like refunding orders or sending emails. My job as a tester is to check what actually happened, not what the AI said happened.

Every reply sounded fine.
Only 72% actually did the right thing.

I built a small AI customer-support agent for a pretend shop. It can look up orders, issue refunds or hand a case to a human. Then I built an "exam room" around it:

  1. 12 real-world scenarios, each with a correct answer
  2. Every scenario run 5 times, because AI isn't consistent
  3. Graded on the shop's final state, not the AI's words
  4. Runs fully on my laptop with a small open model, at zero cloud cost
