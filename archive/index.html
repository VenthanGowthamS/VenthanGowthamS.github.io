Everything built so far

Each version of this site was built in conversation with an AI, then reviewed, tested and improved. Newest first: here's how the idea evolved.

  1. Nov 2026 · current

    2126: one site for everyone

    The agent core is a sphere of light, and seven planets of work orbit it. Visitors pick a lens (everyone, leader or engineer), and the whole site rewrites itself at that level. Includes a plain readable page, a dated log, a console, and two invented languages: Orbic and Ora.

    The idea: make it attractive, but never confusing.

    Open
  2. Nov 2026

    3100: the first solar system

    The sun is a revolving sphere that turns into a human face when it speaks. Details unfold in layers: planet, summary, item.

    The idea: in the far future you pull ideas into orbit instead of browsing pages.

    Open
  3. Nov 2026

    2050: holographic and colourless

    A human head made of light evolves from Neanderthal to Homo sapiens to a digital self. My GitHub is shown as a timeline of eras.

    The idea: humans won by tools, judgement, knowledge, stories and images, and so does my work.

    Open
  4. Nov 2026

    Orb: an agent with hands

    A glowing orb reaches out to Build, Evaluate, Read, Speak and Watch. The bookshelf idea started here.

    The idea: make the portfolio clear for non-technical visitors.

    Open
  5. Nov 2026 · first version

    Terminal: talk to my digital twin

    The first version: an orb avatar with an agent terminal you can type into. Inspired by a friend's portfolio.

    The idea: a website you talk to, not just read.

    Open
